How names get in

A company joins this list only if it passes every test:

  • It has been public for at least 3 years.
  • Return on capital averages at least 15% over 5 years, and no single year is under 8%.
  • Profits turn into cash: free cash flow is at least 0.8 times net income.
  • Debt stays low: net debt under 2 times EBITDA, and interest covered 8 times.
  • Assets are not growing in a reckless way.
  • No accounting or bankruptcy red flags.
  • Boom-bust chip names are judged over 7 years, not a short hot streak.
  • The disruption check is done, and it is not red.

Grades judge the company only, never the price. Each account uses its own price rules.

No more than 10% in one stock, and no more than 30% in one theme. Yellow means half size.
